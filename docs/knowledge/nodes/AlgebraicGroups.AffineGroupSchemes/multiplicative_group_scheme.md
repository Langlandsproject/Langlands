# Multiplicative Group Scheme \(\mathbb{G}_m\)

Let \(S\) be a scheme. The **multiplicative group scheme over \(S\)**,
denoted \(\mathbb{G}_{m,S}\), is defined by base change:

\[
\mathbb{G}_{m,S} \;=\; S \,\times_{\operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}}\, \operatorname{Spec} \mathbb{Z}[T, T^{-1}],
\]

where \(\mathbb{Z}[T, T^{-1}]\) is the Laurent polynomial ring (the
localization of \(\mathbb{Z}[T]\) at the multiplicative set generated by
\(T\)). Its coordinate Hopf algebra is \(\mathbb{Z}[T, T^{-1}]\) with the
**multiplicative Hopf structure**:

\[
\Delta(T) = T \otimes T, \qquad
\varepsilon(T) = 1, \qquad
S(T) = T^{-1}.
\]

The structure morphism \(\mathbb{G}_{m,S} \to S\) is affine and locally
of finite type. Hence \(\mathbb{G}_{m,S}\) is an
[[node:affine_group_schemes.algebraic_group_definition|algebraic group]]
over \(S\); it is smooth of relative dimension one and is commutative.
Its functor of points is identified by
[[node:affine_group_schemes.multiplicative_group_scheme_points|the multiplicative points formula]].

The group scheme \(\mathbb{G}_m\) is the rank-one case of the family of
[[node:linear_algebraic_groups.basic_examples|split tori]] \(T = \mathbb{G}_m^n\);
its character lattice is \(X^*(\mathbb{G}_m) \cong \mathbb{Z}\), generated
by the identity character \(T\).
