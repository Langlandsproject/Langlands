# Split-Anisotropic Decomposition of a Torus

Let \(T\) be a torus over \(k\). Then \(T\) contains

- a unique maximal split subtorus \(T_d\), with
  \(X_*(T_d) = X_*(T)^{\Theta}\), equivalently with character lattice
  the maximal torsion-free quotient of \(X^*(T)_{\Theta}\); and
- a unique maximal anisotropic subtorus \(T_a\), with
  \(X^*(T_a) = X^*(T)/X^*(T)^{\Theta}\);

and the multiplication map
\[
T_d \times T_a \longrightarrow T
\]
is an isogeny: it is surjective with finite kernel, so
\(T = T_d \cdot T_a\) with \(T_d \cap T_a\) finite. The decomposition
is an **almost direct product only**: in general
\(T \not\cong T_d \times T_a\).

**Counterexample to directness.** Let \(E/k\) be a separable quadratic
extension and \(T = R_{E/k}\mathbb{G}_m\) (see
[[node:tori.weil_restriction_of_tori]]). Then \(T_d = \mathbb{G}_m\)
(embedded diagonally), \(T_a\) is the norm-one torus
[[node:tori.norm_one_torus]], and \(T_d \cap T_a = \mu_2\). On
character lattices, with \(\sigma\) the non-trivial element of
\(\operatorname{Gal}(E/k)\) acting through the quadratic quotient:
\(X^*(T) = \mathbb{Z}e_1 \oplus \mathbb{Z}e_2\) with
\(\sigma\) swapping \(e_1, e_2\), while
\(X^*(T_d \times T_a) = \mathbb{Z}(1) \oplus \mathbb{Z}(\mathrm{sgn})\).
The swap module contains no basis of eigenvectors (its eigenvector
sublattice \(\mathbb{Z}(e_1{+}e_2) \oplus \mathbb{Z}(e_1{-}e_2)\) has
index \(2\)), so the two \(\Theta\)-lattices are not isomorphic and
the tori are not isomorphic.

*Proof.*
Write \(L = X^*(T)\).

*Existence and shape of \(T_a\).* The invariant sublattice
\(L^{\Theta}\) is saturated in \(L\): if \(n\chi\) is
\(\Theta\)-fixed with \(n \geq 1\) then \(n(\gamma\chi - \chi) = 0\),
so \(\gamma \chi = \chi\) as \(L\) is torsion-free. By
[[node:tori.subtori_and_quotients]] the sublattice \(L^\Theta\) yields
a subtorus \(T_a = H_{L^\Theta}\) with
\(X^*(T_a) = L/L^{\Theta}\). It is anisotropic:
\(\Theta\) acts through a finite quotient \(Q\), and the exact
sequence \(0 \to L^Q \to L^Q \to (L/L^Q)^Q \to H^1(Q, L^Q)\) obtained
from \(0 \to L^Q \to L \to L/L^Q \to 0\) begins with the identity map,
while \(H^1(Q, L^Q) = \operatorname{Hom}(Q, L^Q) = 0\) because \(Q\)
is finite and \(L^Q\) torsion-free; hence \((L/L^{Q})^{Q} = 0\). Any
anisotropic subtorus \(S \subseteq T\) has character lattice a
quotient \(L/L'\) with \((L/L')^\Theta = 0\); the image of
\(L^{\Theta}\) in \(L/L'\) is fixed, hence zero, so
\(L^{\Theta} \subseteq L'\) and \(S \subseteq T_a\): maximality.

*Existence and shape of \(T_d\).* A split subtorus corresponds to a
saturated \(\Theta\)-stable \(L'\) with trivial action on \(L/L'\).
The smallest such \(L'\) is the saturation of the augmentation
sublattice generated by all \(\gamma\chi - \chi\), whose quotient is
the maximal torsion-free quotient of the coinvariants
\(L_{\Theta}\); the corresponding subtorus \(T_d\) is the maximal
split subtorus, and dualizing the surjection
\(L \twoheadrightarrow X^*(T_d)\) gives
\(X_*(T_d) = X_*(T)^{\Theta}\).

*Isogeny.* The map on characters induced by multiplication is
\[
L \longrightarrow X^*(T_d) \oplus X^*(T_a), \qquad
\chi \longmapsto (\chi \bmod{}, \; \chi \bmod L^{\Theta}).
\]
Its kernel consists of \(\chi \in L^{\Theta}\) mapping to torsion in
\(L_{\Theta}\); but for \(\chi\) fixed and non-zero the norm
identification \(L^Q \otimes \mathbb{Q} \cong L_Q \otimes \mathbb{Q}\)
shows its image in \(L_\Theta \otimes \mathbb{Q}\) is non-zero, so the
kernel vanishes. The two sides have equal rank
(\(\operatorname{rk} L = \operatorname{rk} L^Q + \operatorname{rk} L/L^{Q}\)
after tensoring with \(\mathbb{Q}\)), so the cokernel is finite.
An injection of \(\Theta\)-lattices with finite cokernel corresponds
to an isogeny of tori by
[[node:tori.f_tori_galois_module_classification]].
