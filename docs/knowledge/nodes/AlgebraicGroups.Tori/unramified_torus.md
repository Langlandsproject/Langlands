# Unramified Torus

Let \(k\) be a Henselian discretely valued field (see
[[node:foundations.local_fields]]), \(K \subseteq k_s\) its
maximal unramified extension, and
\(I = \operatorname{Gal}(k_s/K) \subseteq \Theta\) the inertia
subgroup.

A torus \(T\) over \(k\) is **unramified** if \(T_K\) is
[[node:tori.split_torus|split]]. Equivalently:

- the inertia group \(I\) acts trivially on \(X^*(T)\); equivalently
- \(T\) splits over a finite unramified extension of \(k\)
  (by [[node:tori.splitting_field]] the minimal splitting field of an
  unramified torus is a finite subextension of \(K/k\)).

For an unramified torus the Galois-lattice data reduces to an action
of the unramified quotient \(\operatorname{Gal}(K/k)\) on
\(X^*(T)\); over a finite or, more generally, quasi-finite residue
field this quotient is (topologically generated by) Frobenius, so
unramified tori are classified by a single automorphism of the
lattice up to conjugacy.
