# Affine Weyl Groups

The **affine Weyl group** of a reductive group is the semidirect
product of its finite Weyl group with the coroot lattice. It is the
Coxeter group governing the affine Bruhat-Tits building, and the
basic combinatorial structure on the affine flag variety and the
Iwahori-Hecke algebra.

## Definition

Let \((W, S)\) be the finite Weyl group of a root system
\(\Phi \subseteq V\) with coroots \(\Phi^\vee\). The **affine Weyl
group** is the semidirect product
\[
W_{\mathrm{aff}} \;:=\; W \;\ltimes\; \mathbb{Z}\Phi^\vee,
\]
acting on \(V\) by:
\[
(w, \lambda) \cdot v \;=\; w(v) + \lambda.
\]

Equivalently, \(W_{\mathrm{aff}}\) is the group generated by the
finite reflections \(s_\alpha\) (\(\alpha \in \Phi\)) together with
the **affine reflection**
\[
s_0 : v \mapsto v - (\langle v, \alpha_0^\vee \rangle - 1) \alpha_0,
\]
where \(\alpha_0\) is the highest root.

## Extended affine Weyl group

For applications to non-simply-connected groups, the **extended affine
Weyl group** is
\[
\tilde W \;:=\; W \;\ltimes\; X_*,
\]
where \(X_*\) is the cocharacter lattice (containing \(\mathbb{Z}\Phi^\vee\)
as a sublattice). The quotient
\[
\Omega \;:=\; \tilde W / W_{\mathrm{aff}} \;=\; X_* / \mathbb{Z}\Phi^\vee \;=\; \pi_1(G)
\]
acts as **diagram automorphisms** on the affine Dynkin diagram.

For \(G\) simply connected: \(X_* = \mathbb{Z}\Phi^\vee\), so
\(\Omega = 1\) and \(\tilde W = W_{\mathrm{aff}}\).

## Coxeter structure

\(W_{\mathrm{aff}}\) is a Coxeter group with generators
\(\{s_0\} \cup S\), where:

- \(S = \{s_1, \ldots, s_r\}\) are the simple reflections of \(W\);
- \(s_0\) is the affine reflection through the wall of the
  fundamental alcove opposite to the origin.

The braid relations include the new ones \((s_0 s_i)^{m_{0i}} = 1\)
encoded by the **affine Dynkin diagram** \(\tilde \Phi\).

The extended affine Weyl group \(\tilde W\) is **not** a Coxeter group
in general, but \(\Omega\) acts by automorphisms of the Coxeter system.

## Alcoves

The fundamental domain for \(W_{\mathrm{aff}}\) acting on \(V\) is the
**fundamental alcove**:
\[
A_0 \;=\; \{v \in V : 0 \le \langle \alpha_i, v \rangle, \langle \alpha_0, v \rangle \le 1\}.
\]

The \(W_{\mathrm{aff}}\)-translates of \(A_0\) tile \(V\) into
**alcoves**, and \(W_{\mathrm{aff}}\) acts simply transitively on
alcoves.

For \(\tilde W\), the action of \(\Omega\) permutes alcoves within an
\(\Omega\)-orbit (= "stripe").

## Examples

### Type \(\tilde A_1\)

\(W = \mathbb{Z}/2\), \(\Phi^\vee = \mathbb{Z}\), so
\(W_{\mathrm{aff}} = \mathbb{Z}/2 \ltimes \mathbb{Z} = D_\infty\)
(infinite dihedral). Alcoves = intervals \([n, n+1] \subseteq \mathbb{R}\).

\(X_* = \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{Z}\) for \(\mathrm{PGL}_2\), so
\(\Omega = \mathbb{Z}/2\) and \(\tilde W = (\mathbb{Z}/2) \ltimes \frac{1}{2}\mathbb{Z}\).

### Type \(\tilde A_{n-1}\)

\(W = S_n\), \(\mathbb{Z}\Phi^\vee = \{(a_1, \ldots, a_n) : \sum a_i = 0\}\).
The affine Dynkin diagram is a cycle of \(n\) nodes. Alcoves = top
simplices in the triangulation of \(\{v \in \mathbb{R}^n : \sum v_i = 0\}\)
by hyperplanes \(v_i - v_j \in \mathbb{Z}\).

For \(\mathrm{GL}_n\): \(X_* = \mathbb{Z}^n\),
\(\Omega = \mathbb{Z}\) (the determinant lattice), generated by the
"cyclic rotation" of the affine diagram.

### Type \(\tilde G_2\)

\(W\) = dihedral of order 12. Affine Dynkin = "tail" attached to the
finite \(G_2\) diagram. \(\Omega = 1\).

## Length function

For \(\tilde w = w \cdot t_\lambda \in \tilde W\):
\[
\ell(\tilde w) \;=\; \sum_{\alpha \in \Phi^+} \big| \langle \alpha, \lambda \rangle + \chi(w^{-1}\alpha < 0) \big|,
\]
where \(\chi\) is the indicator. This generalises the finite Weyl
group length and is the foundation for **Bruhat order** on
\(W_{\mathrm{aff}}\).

## Bruhat decomposition for \(G(F)\)

For \(G\) reductive over a local field \(F\):
\[
G(F) \;=\; \bigsqcup_{\tilde w \in \tilde W} I \cdot \tilde w \cdot I,
\]
where \(I\) is the Iwahori subgroup. This is the **affine Bruhat
decomposition** and the source of the Iwahori-Hecke algebra.

## Role in Langlands

- **Affine flag variety** \(\mathcal{F}\ell_G = G(F)/I\) is
  stratified by \(\tilde W\); each stratum is an affine space.
- **Affine Grassmannian** \(\mathrm{Gr}_G = G(F)/G(\mathcal{O})\) is
  stratified by \(\tilde W / W = X_*\); the closures of strata are
  **affine Schubert varieties**.
- **Iwahori-Hecke algebra** \(\mathcal{H}(\tilde W) = \mathbb{Z}[v, v^{-1}][\tilde W]\)
  with quadratic relations; the affine Hecke algebra of \(G\).
- **Kazhdan-Lusztig**: Kazhdan-Lusztig polynomials of \(\tilde W\)
  control local-Langlands branching and category O at non-integral
  weights.
- **Geometric Satake**: equivalence of categories
  \(\mathrm{Perv}_{G(\mathcal{O})}(\mathrm{Gr}_G) \cong \mathrm{Rep}(\hat G)\)
  is parametrised by \(X_*^+ = X_*\) (dominant cocharacters).
