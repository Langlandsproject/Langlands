# Chevalley Isomorphism Theorem

Let \(G\) and \(G'\) be connected reductive groups over an algebraically
closed field, with maximal tori \(T\subseteq G\) and \(T'\subseteq G'\). An
isomorphism of root data
\[
(X^*(T),\Phi(G,T),X_*(T),\Phi^\vee(G,T))
\;\cong\;
(X^*(T'),\Phi(G',T'),X_*(T'),\Phi^\vee(G',T'))
\]
is induced, after choosing compatible root subgroup parametrizations, by an
isomorphism of algebraic groups
\[
G \xrightarrow{\sim} G'
\]
carrying \(T\) onto \(T'\).

In particular, a connected reductive group over an algebraically closed field
is determined up to isomorphism by its root datum together with the usual
pinning choices needed to remove automorphisms.

*Proof strategy.*  
The root datum identifies the maximal tori and matches each root
\(\alpha\) with a corresponding root \(\alpha'\). Choose compatible
isomorphisms \(U_\alpha\simeq U'_{\alpha'}\) for the root subgroups. The
rank-one commutator relations among \(T\) and the \(U_\alpha\) are determined
by the root datum and the chosen parameters. Therefore the assignments on
\(T\) and on all \(U_\alpha\) respect the defining relations.

Since a connected reductive group is generated by its maximal torus and root
subgroups, these compatible assignments extend to a homomorphism
\(G\to G'\). Applying the same construction to the inverse root-datum
isomorphism gives an inverse homomorphism. The full proof is the detailed
verification that the Chevalley commutator constants and rank-one
normalizations can be chosen compatibly.
